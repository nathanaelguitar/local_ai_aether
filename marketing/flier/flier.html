Your AI shouldn't cost the planet.

Most chatbots live in warehouse-size data centers that burn electricity 24/7 and guzzle water to stay cool. CanopyChat lives on your phone — just your phone. No extra warehouses. Private by default.

    No water wastedServer farms use millions of gallons for cooling. Canopy keeps its cool in your pocket.

    Climate-contributingA percentage of revenue goes to permanent carbon removal via Stripe Climate.

    Private by defaultHistory stays on your phone unless you choose to share it.
